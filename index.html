What is PlayEvento?

We bring play to the event.

PlayEvento is an event entertainment brand bringing interactive games, recreational experiences and playful activities to the places where people gather.

From birthdays and family get-togethers to schools, societies, corporate events, festivals and pop-ups, we offer a curated range of experiences designed to get people playing, competing, exploring and having fun together.

Whether you are planning a small gathering or a large event, PlayEvento makes it easy to add engaging entertainment to the occasion.
